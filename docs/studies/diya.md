# Diya motion study
Scope: one isolated study at /diya-study.html. The five-card carousel is unchanged.

Source: user-provided Zsolt Kacso reference, 2880 × 2160, 30 fps.
Observed: center capsule approximately 464 × 740; white-hot flame base overlaps the upper cap; soft red tongues move independently; sparse points and hollow triangles; low rim light; a faint floor reflection. The earlier implementation's narrow plume, flat surface and uniform fades did not reproduce this.

This study explicitly uses a reference-derived fire video texture, not an independently generated fluid simulation. It isolates the stable section at 15.25–16.50 seconds, overlapping the last 0.20 seconds with 15.05–15.25 to hide the loop seam. A capsule matte removes the source card and lettering. The source's full card is visible only in the labeled comparison pane.

The Diya surface is reconstructed as a smooth color field from 30 palette samples; the icon, text, capsule, rim, stage and interaction are drawn separately. Flame ignition starts at 150 ms, builds over 420 ms; extinguishing takes 380 ms and embers persist for 650 ms. Playback can be paused or slowed; reduced motion starts paused. Both comparison assets use the same source interval and crop geometry.

Review required: this is a quality study for user judgment, not a claim of an original procedural match or a rollout to the other cards.
